What is a sponsored post?

Your product appears as a deal in the CommonCents feed, just like the deals our readers browse every day — with a clear "Sponsored" label on it, so readers always know it's a paid placement.

How it works

  1. Send us your product. Reach out through the contact form with your product and its Amazon link.
  2. We set up tracking just for you. Your post gets a dedicated tag under our Amazon account, so you can see exactly what your post sold — or we use your own affiliate program's link, if you run one.
  3. We check the price, then it goes live. A sponsored post still has to be a genuine deal. Once the price checks out, your deal goes live in the feed.
