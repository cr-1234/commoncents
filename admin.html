Deal views & clicks

Two different signals (counted since Nov 12, 2026 — views started a few hours after clicks): Views = someone opened the deal's window — pure interest; that's what decides whether a deal earns its place on the site. Clicks to Amazon = someone tapped "View Deal" / "Get This Deal" and went to the store — those are the ones that can turn into sales. Most-viewed first — the deals at the bottom are the ones nobody's even opening.
