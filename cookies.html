Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2026. This Cookie Policy explains how CommonCents uses cookies and local browser storage technologies to enhance your online user experience.

1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies and local storage are small text files downloaded to your device when you load websites. They allow our static site to remember details from your last visit locally in your browser.

2. How We Use Them

We use browser storage to provide functional consistency:
• Your Feed Picks: The deal categories you pick for your tailored feed, so "My Feed" shows the kinds of deals you chose.
• Your Votes: Which deals you've voted up or down, so your votes stay in place between visits.
• Your Feed View: The last feed view you chose — My Feed or a browse category — so the site reopens on the view you left it on.
• Feed Picker: Remembers that you've finished picking your feed categories, so the picker doesn't pop open again on every visit.
• Last Visit: The date of your last visit, so deals posted since then can be marked "NEW FOR YOU".
• Deals You've Opened: The deals you've clicked into, so their "NEW FOR YOU" mark goes away once you've seen them.
• Giveaway Tracking: The email you entered the weekly giveaway with, the week you entered, the latest entrant count, and which days you've visited this week, so your entry and any bonus entries are counted correctly.
• Sign-In Session: If you sign in, Firebase — the service that runs our accounts — keeps your sign-in session in local storage so you stay signed in between visits.

3. Affiliate Tracking Cookies

Third-party partner stores (like Amazon) use standard tracking cookies to record referral sales from CommonCents links, so we earn advertising commissions.

4. Managing Cookies

You can clear cookies and local storage at any time via your browser settings. Restricting them may reset your saved feed picks, votes, and feed view, sign you out, or require re-entering active weekly drawing entries.
