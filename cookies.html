Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2026. This Cookie Policy explains how CommonCents uses cookies and local browser storage technologies to enhance your online user experience.

1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies and local storage are small text files downloaded to your device when you load websites. They allow our static site to remember details from your last visit locally in your browser.

2. How We Use Them

We use browser storage to provide functional consistency:
• Your Feed Picks: The deal categories you pick for your tailored feed, so "My Feed" shows the kinds of deals you chose.
• Deals Audience: Whether you asked to see deals for everyone, for men, or for women, so deals clearly geared to the other audience stay out of your feed.
• Your Votes: Which deals you've voted up or down, so your votes stay in place between visits. Small timestamps of when you voted — and when you removed a vote — are kept alongside, so a vote you removed stays removed when your account syncs.
• Saved Deals: The deals you bookmark with the Save button (stored under the key ccMySaves) — a small snapshot of each deal (its title, price, picture, and store link) so your saved list is still here when you come back, even if a deal later leaves the site. If you sign in, the same list is also kept on your account so it follows you across devices. A small note of when you removed a saved deal is kept alongside, so a deal you unsaved stays unsaved when your account syncs.
• Your Feed View: The last feed view you chose — My Feed or a browse category — so the site reopens on the view you left it on.
• Feed Picker: Remembers that you've finished picking your feed categories, so the picker doesn't pop open again on every visit.
• Last Visit: The date of your last visit, so deals posted since then can be marked "NEW DEAL".
• New Deal Marks: Which deals still carry their "NEW DEAL" mark. A mark stays on a deal until you open that deal — opening one deal never removes another deal's mark.
• Deals You've Opened: The deals you've clicked into, so their "NEW DEAL" mark goes away once you've seen them. If you sign in, this list is also kept on your account, so a deal you opened on one device doesn't still say "NEW DEAL" on another.
• Freebies You've Opened: The freebies you've clicked into, so a freebie carries a "NEW" mark until you open it — the same rule as deals, and it follows your account the same way when you sign in.
• Giveaway Tracking: The email you entered the weekly giveaway with, the week you entered, the latest entrant count, and which days you've visited this week, so your entry and any bonus entries are counted correctly.
• Sign-In Session: If you sign in, Firebase — the service that runs our accounts — keeps your sign-in session in local storage so you stay signed in between visits.
• Notification Hand-Off: If you tap a notification on a page that can't open the deal it points to, the deal's ID is held in session storage (key ccOpenNote) just long enough to open it when you arrive back on the home page. Session storage is erased automatically when you close the tab.

3. Affiliate Tracking Cookies

Third-party partner stores (like Amazon) use standard tracking cookies to record referral sales from CommonCents links, so we earn advertising commissions.

4. Managing Cookies

You can clear cookies and local storage at any time via your browser settings. Restricting them may reset your saved feed picks, votes, saved deals, and feed view, sign you out, or require re-entering active weekly drawing entries.
