Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. At CommonCents, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data collected from you. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information when you interact with our website.

1. Information We Collect

When you participate in our weekly drawing giveaways or submit comments, we collect your email address. This information is used to keep track of entries, verify winners, and prevent duplicate submissions. If you create an optional CommonCents account (with an email and password, or through Google sign-in), we also store your email address and the deal categories you pick for your tailored feed, so your preferences are saved to your account. If you sign up for deal emails, we store your email address so we can send them.

2. Cookies & Local Storage

We use standard browser local storage and cookies to maintain your preferences (such as dark mode theme, search terms, and customized submitted deal cards). For visitors who browse without an account, this information stays on your device and is not uploaded to our servers. If you create an account, the categories you pick for your tailored feed are saved to your account instead, as described above.

3. Affiliate Links

CommonCents links out to external shopping websites, such as Amazon, using affiliate tracking links. When you purchase via these links, the store records our tracking ID to provide us with credit for referring the sale.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your email address or comments to any third-party marketing companies. We use a small number of service providers to run the site: Google Firebase stores accounts and giveaway entries and handles sign-in, and Brevo sends our emails (such as giveaway confirmations and winner notices). These providers process your information only so we can operate these features. You can ask us to delete your account and stored information at any time through our Contact page.
